Three quiet days on a lake in Canada

No phone signal, no traffic, just a canoe, a small cabin and a lake surrounded by pine trees. Sara spent her mornings paddling and her evenings by the fire, and says it was the most relaxing trip she's ever taken.

The best part? The whole trip cost less than a weekend in a big city.

Packing light

One carry-on is enough for almost any trip. Pick clothes that match each other, bring layers instead of heavy jackets and leave the "just in case" items at home.

A small daypack, a reusable water bottle and comfortable shoes will matter more than anything else in your bag.

Travel slower

Trying to see five cities in a week usually means seeing none of them properly. Stay longer in fewer places, talk to locals and leave some days with no plans at all.

Some of the best memories come from the days you didn't plan.
